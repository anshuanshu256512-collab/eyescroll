Control the cursor with your eyes

  • Camera required — everything runs locally, nothing is uploaded
  • Fast calibration — 9 dots, ~25 seconds. Tracks iris movement precisely
  • 3-axis head compensation — auto-corrects for tilt, nod & turn even after calibration
Best results: Sit 50–70 cm from the screen in good, even lighting. Avoid strong backlight (a bright window behind you hurts accuracy).

Calibration Rules

1 Place your device flat or prop it so the camera faces your face directly
2 Keep your head completely still throughout — only your eyes should move
3 A dot will appear on screen. Look at its exact centre and keep your gaze there until it moves
4 When the dot slides to the next spot, follow it smoothly with your eyes
5 Do 9 points in total — about 25 seconds
